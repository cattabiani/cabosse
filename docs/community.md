# Community and partners

Suggestions from colleagues, recorded as received (2026-10-08). Nothing here
is a decision, and the facts below are not yet verified. An ASIC is out of
scope for now (D-036); the silicon notes are kept for later.

## Levels of openness

- Open code (RTL, software) is possible today. Example: the PULP platform
  at ETH Zurich.
- An open full chip also needs a foundry whose process details can be
  published. Most processes are under NDA.

## Open processes and shuttles

A shuttle (multi-project wafer, MPW) shares one production run between
many small designs, which cuts the cost.

| Process          | Origin (as given) | Shuttle      |
|------------------|-------------------|--------------|

| GF 180 nm        | TW (to verify)    | wafer.space  |
| IHP SG13G2       | DE                | IHP          |

## Communities

- Free Silicon Foundation and its Free Silicon Conference.
- FOSSI Foundation: several conferences a year (ORConf among them).
  Channels: <https://fossi-foundation.org/get-involved>.
- HeiChips (Heidelberg University): yearly hackathon.

## Possible partners and contacts

- Heidelberg University, Dirk Koch: FABulous, a framework for building
  custom FPGA fabrics. Idea raised: a custom FPGA with hard blocks for the
  accelerator where it matters.
- Borg: an open GPU that has been taped out.
- Fractile: European AI accelerator company; sponsored ORConf this year.
- EPFL: Andreas Toftegaard Kristensen, who built a neural network
