## The engine

`rtl/engine.sv`: 4 lanes per memory port, all lanes in lockstep on one
control, a DMA engine and a FIFO per port. It runs `MATVEC`, `SCORES` and
`VALUES`.

<!-- begin: shape -->
<!-- end: shape -->

- **Stages (D-041):** issue (DMA commands), fill (attention's beats into
  the tile buffer), step (a beat to every lane per cycle) and drain (8
  results per cycle to the write port, scaled for `SCORES`, rounded for a
  BF16 destination). Each has its own command queue, so back-to-back
  commands leave no gap. Issue, step and drain walk the same sequence of
  units: a `MATVEC` pass is a `SCORES` tile of one head whose positions
  are rows.
- **Read-ahead (D-042):** a command whose input is not ready yet comes
  with `wait`; its weights (or its KV tiles) are read at once, and it
  steps on its go.
- **KV cache (D-043, D-044):** position `p` of a KV head sits on channel
  `p mod 32`, so a 128-position tile is one 16-beat read per port.
  Attention reads the cache only after the `KV_STORE` that writes it is
  done. A tile serves one query head and is read once (Q-27 is about
  sharing it across a group).
- **Tile buffer:** 128 banks with rotations, so the fill, `SCORES` (a
  position per lane) and `VALUES` (a dimension per lane) each take one
  rotation per cycle; two slots, so the next tile fills while the current
  one steps.
- **Controller (D-045, for M7):** commands are handed over in order, and
  the controller stops at a non-engine command until its inputs are
  written. `commands.hazards()` checks the ordering rules on the command
  lists.

Two things the measurements changed in the perf model:

- **Tile wait:** a p.V unit fills its two heads' values for a whole tile
  but steps only the positions in it, so a partly filled last tile waits
  for its fill (`perf.tile_wait_cols`). Full tiles and `SCORES` never wait.
- **Start-up:** a waiting command takes `engine_startup_cycles` over its
  engine cycles once its go comes: the most measured over every command
  kind (design v0).

## FIFO depth

Each port's FIFO must cover the memory's latency and the other ports'
random pauses, since the lanes step in lockstep. Rate as a share of a
1024-beat FIFO's, 32 ports, F2's latency, memory seeds 1 to 5. The
default is 128 beats, provisional until M8 measures F2's HBM (192 if its
pauses look like the harsher column).

## Tests

- `verif/tests/test_engine.py` (Verilator driver `verif/bulk/engine.cpp`,
  memory model `verif/bulk/axi_mem.h`): at 4, 8 and 20 ports in CI, 32 in
  the wide job. Every op over row counts around a group and a pass,
  columns around a beat, head dimensions 16 to 128, groups 1, 2, 3 and
  9 / 3, positions around a tile and `t = cap`, NaN in the cache and in
  p past t; six memory behaviours; gos; fill running ahead past
  `MATVEC`s; back-to-back commands at their exact perf-model cycles.
  Slow: SmolLM2 at three positions, and bandwidth.
- `verif/tests/test_port_dma.py`, `test_stream_fifo.py`,
  `test_tile_banks.py`, `test_fp32_to_bf16.py`: the DMA, the FIFO, the
  bank mapping (exhaustive) and the rounder.
- `model/tests/test_commands.py`: the recording hook, the weight layout,
  `hazards()` on every command list, `record_step` from a saved cache;
  `test_long_cache.py`; `test_perf.py`'s tile wait.
- `verif/tests/test_rtl_checks.py`: lint and synthesis of every module,
  the elaboration checks, the logic-depth limits.
- Hand mutations, for each RTL step (listed in PLAN.md and the PRs): all
  caught except ones shown harmless.
- Reviews: `/simplify` and `/code-review` on every PR.

## Open issues

1. **Bandwidth criterion as written.** `down` cannot reach 90% (above).
   The owner decides whether "90% times the perf model's engine use"
   replaces it.
2. **FIFO depth** (128 beats) is provisional until M8 measures F2's HBM
   under load.
3. **No FPGA build yet.** The engine's area and timing at 250 MHz come
   with M8's Vivado run; until then only the Yosys proxy. The four stage
   queues each hold the whole command; M8's utilization report says
   whether synthesis drops the unread fields.
4. **K and V are read once per query head** (Q-27): free while the
   memory keeps up with the engine, at most about 8% on attention at F2's
   rate (estimate).
5. **`VALUES` runs at most 2 heads a pass.** At 256 lanes (M9) half the
   lanes idle on p.V; raise `Heads` to L / d there.
6. **Scores and probabilities at stride `cap`.** The golden command list
   still packs them (rows of t); M6/M7 move it to the engine's layout,
   and the test's re-striding goes.
7. **For M7:** the controller's stop at a waiting non-engine command
   (D-045) may cost some read-ahead, at most about 1.8% of a token at
   position 63 (estimate); M7 measures it. A simulated token will take
   minutes (the speed above).
8. **Memory traffic is not counted by the tests:** a `MATVEC` reading
   every port (a hand mutation) gives the same bits and cycles.
