## What was built

- `docs/numerics.md`: the numerics spec. BF16 × BF16 products, FP32
  accumulation and vector unit, FMA everywhere, and the summation order of
  every reduction.
- `model/golden/`: the golden model, written as the hardware will compute.
  - Arithmetic primitives with exact-oracle tests (PR #1).
  - rsqrt, reciprocal and exp approximations (PR #2).
  - The dot product with `A` interleaved accumulators and a pairwise tree
    (PR #3).
  - Reductions, RMSNorm, softmax (PR #5), RoPE, SiLU/SwiGLU (PR #6).
  - The decoder: checkpoint weights as published, a flat KV cache, the decode
    step, and tiny random-weight configs (PR #7).
- `model/compare.py`: comparison against `transformers` in FP32 and in BF16
  (PR #8).
- Greedy-decode regression fixtures for a tiny config and for SmolLM2 (PR #9).
- `REVIEW.md`: the review checklist agents follow (PR #4).

Decisions taken during M1: D-019 (FMA), D-020 (function approximations), D-021
(hosting and CI), D-022 (softmax without online rescaling), D-023 (reference
platform), D-024 (flat KV cache).

## Open issues

- **Provisional parameters.** The number of accumulators `A` and the
  reduction width `S` are placeholders until the lane design in M2 fixes
  them. Changing them changes the golden model's bits, and the fixtures must
  be regenerated.
- **The fixtures don't see `S`.** A change of `S` changes some FP32 sums by
  one ulp, and the BF16 rounding of the RMSNorm and softmax outputs absorbs
  it. The per-operation tests in `test_vector.py` do catch it. A possible
  follow-up for M7 is per-layer hashes of the FP32 values before rounding.
- **Pinned values are checked on the reference platform only (D-023).** On
  other platforms the RoPE tables are checked to within 1 ulp, and the pinned
  hashes are skipped.
- **Golden-model speed.** Fine for M1. Optimisations that change neither the
  bits nor the spec remain possible if later milestones need more tokens.
- **Open questions carried into M2** (PLAN.md): Q-10 (HBM ports), Q-11 (p·V
  layout), Q-12 (controller), Q-13 (F2 shell), Q-22 (accumulators or rotated
  rows). Q-08 asks whether the function approximations hold end to end. The
  comparison above is that check, so it can be closed if you agree.
- **Later:** Q-24 (circular or paged KV cache), only with a model trained for
  longer contexts.
